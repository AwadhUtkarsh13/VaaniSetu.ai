## 1. Scheme Overview

### 1.1 Objective
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme that provides income support to all landholding farmer families in the country, so they can meet expenses related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs. The scheme is 100% funded by the Government of India and administered by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.

### 1.2 Launch and effective date
The scheme was launched on 24 February 2019 at Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, with effect from 1 December 2018. The first installment covered the period December 2018 – March 2019.

### 1.3 Role of States and Union Territories
State Governments and UT Administrations identify eligible farmer families according to the scheme guidelines, verify land records, and upload beneficiary data to the PM-KISAN portal. The Central Government transfers the benefit directly to the beneficiaries' bank accounts.

## 2. Benefits

### 2.1 Amount of benefit
An eligible farmer family receives ₹6,000 per year, paid in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each, every four months.

### 2.2 Installment periods
The three installment periods in each financial year are: April–July, August–November, and December–March. A family registered in a given period becomes entitled to the installment of that period, subject to successful verification.

### 2.3 Mode of payment
The benefit is transferred through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the bank account of the beneficiary. The account must be seeded with Aadhaar and mapped on the NPCI payment platform. No cash or intermediary is involved at any stage, and no fee is payable by the farmer to receive the benefit.

## 3. Eligibility

### 3.1 Definition of a farmer family
A "farmer family" means a family comprising husband, wife and minor children who together own cultivable land as per the land records of the concerned State or UT. Benefit is provided per family, not per individual landowner.

### 3.2 Coverage irrespective of landholding size
The scheme was initially limited to small and marginal farmer families holding up to 2 hectares of cultivable land. With effect from 1 June 2019 it was extended to all landholding farmer families irrespective of the size of their landholding, subject to the exclusion criteria in Section 4.

### 3.3 Land ownership cut-off date
The cut-off date for determining eligibility is 1 February 2019: the family's name should appear in the land records as on that date. Ownership acquired after the cut-off date does not make a family eligible, except where land is transferred by succession on the death of the landowner.

### 3.4 Landless and tenant cultivators
Because the benefit is linked to ownership of cultivable land in land records, landless agricultural labourers, tenant farmers, sharecroppers and cultivators on leased land are not eligible under PM-KISAN unless they themselves own cultivable land recorded in their name.

### 3.5 Special arrangements
In North-Eastern States where land ownership is community-based, in forest areas, and in Jharkhand where land records are not updated, alternate implementation mechanisms approved by a committee of the State Government apply. Forest-rights holders recognised under the Forest Rights Act are also covered.

## 4. Exclusion Criteria
Families in which one or more members belong to any of the following categories are not eligible, even if they own cultivable land.

### 4.1 Institutional landholders
All institutional landholders (for example trusts, companies, cooperative societies and government or religious institutions holding land) are excluded.

### 4.2 Holders of constitutional posts
Former and present holders of constitutional posts are excluded.

### 4.3 Elected representatives and ministers
Former and present Ministers and State Ministers, former and present Members of the Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, State Legislative Assemblies and State Legislative Councils, former and present Mayors of Municipal Corporations, and former and present Chairpersons of District Panchayats are excluded.

### 4.4 Government employees
All serving or retired officers and employees of Central or State Government Ministries, Offices and Departments and their field units, Central or State Public Sector Enterprises, attached offices and autonomous institutions under Government, and regular employees of Local Bodies are excluded. Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D employees are NOT excluded.

### 4.5 Pensioners
All superannuated or retired pensioners whose monthly pension is ₹10,000 or more are excluded. Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D retirees are NOT excluded.

### 4.6 Income tax payers
All persons who paid income tax in the last assessment year are excluded. If any member of the farmer family (husband, wife or minor child) paid income tax, the entire family is ineligible.

### 4.7 Registered professionals
Professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects who are registered with professional bodies and carry out their profession are excluded.

## 5. Registration and Enrolment

### 5.1 How to register
A farmer can register through the State-nominated nodal officer, patwari or revenue officer, or the local agriculture office; through a Common Service Centre (CSC); or by self-registration under "Farmers Corner" on the PM-KISAN portal or the PM-KISAN mobile app. Registration is free of cost.

### 5.2 Documents required
Registration requires the Aadhaar number of the farmer, details of an Aadhaar-seeded bank account, the land record details (khasra/khatauni or equivalent) of cultivable land, and a mobile number.

### 5.3 Verification by the State
After self-registration the application is verified by the State or UT Government against land records. Only applications approved by the State are forwarded for payment.

## 6. Mandatory Requirements for Release of Installments

### 6.1 eKYC
eKYC is mandatory for all PM-KISAN beneficiaries. It can be completed through OTP on the PM-KISAN portal, biometric authentication at a CSC or State Seva Kendra, or face authentication in the PM-KISAN mobile app.

### 6.2 Aadhaar seeding of bank account
The beneficiary's bank account must be seeded with Aadhaar and enabled for DBT. Beneficiaries may open an account with India Post Payments Bank (IPPB), which is Aadhaar-seeded by default.

### 6.3 Land seeding
The beneficiary's land details must be verified and seeded by the State Government in the PM-KISAN database. Installments are withheld if land seeding is pending.

## 7. Payment Status and Grievances

### 7.1 Checking status
Beneficiaries can check their status through "Know Your Status" on the PM-KISAN portal using their registration number, or by looking up their registration number with their mobile or Aadhaar number.

### 7.2 Common reasons for non-receipt of an installment
The most common reasons are: eKYC not completed; bank account not seeded with Aadhaar or not DBT-enabled; land seeding pending; mismatch between the name in Aadhaar and in the bank account or land records; the bank account being closed, frozen or invalid; and rejection during State verification.

### 7.3 Helpline and grievance channels
PM-KISAN helpline numbers are 155261 and 011-24300606, and the e-mail is pmkisan-ict@gov.in. A grievance can be raised under the "Help Desk" on the PM-KISAN portal, with the district or State nodal officer, or on CPGRAMS (https://pgportal.gov.in) addressed to the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.

## 8. Recovery and Penalties

### 8.1 Recovery of wrongful payments
If a beneficiary is found ineligible after receiving benefits, including through false self-declaration, the amount received is liable to be recovered, and the beneficiary may face action under applicable law. Ineligible beneficiaries can voluntarily surrender benefits through the portal.
